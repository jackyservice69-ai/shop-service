# Iya Imagegen v5 assets

Generated on 25 October 2026 using the built-in Imagegen tool at the owner's request.

The hero is an edit of the project's existing `hero-no-button-v3.webp`. Other assets are new generations following Iya's existing blush-pink, ivory and gold theme. Exact prompts and intended use are documented in `docs/IYA-REFRESH-V5-PROMPTS.json` and `docs/IYA-REFRESH-V5.md`.

Fourteen category WebP images (512px square), hero WebP (1440px plus 720px variant), wallet-card background (1100px), bank-panel background (800px), transparent gold heart coin (128px), four announcement JPEGs (1080×1350), and four promotion-concept JPEGs (1200×800). Web derivatives are resized/encoded from the Imagegen outputs with Sharp. Original PNGs remain locally under `output/imagegen/iya-refresh-v5/`.

The payment QR, bank symbols, account information, wallet balance and actual shop logo are never generated or embedded in decorative assets. Campaign images are drafts, without activated offers. Existing bank and brand artwork retains its separate provenance.
