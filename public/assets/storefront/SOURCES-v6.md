# Compact Iya banners v6

The hero-compact-v6 and promotion-compact-v6 WebP images and their small variants were edited with built-in Imagegen on 25 October 2026 at the owner's request. Input assets are the existing Iya hero v5 and promotion-no-button-v3. Final outputs retain the existing wording, pastel palette and shopping/gift objects, without baked-in buttons or white padding bands.

Exact prompts, refinement prompts, sizes, intended use and local source locations are recorded in `docs/COMPACT-BANNERS-V6.md` and `docs/COMPACT-BANNERS-V6-PROMPTS.json`. Web variants are resized/encoded with Sharp from the selected generated PNGs. Full PNGs remain in the local workspace under `output/imagegen/compact-v6/`; prior assets remain intact.

Contact palette v6 was recolored using built-in Imagegen with contact-no-button-v3 as the edit target and the compact v6 Hero/promotion as palette references. The two contact-palette-v6 WebP derivatives are resized/encoded from the selected PNG, retained locally under output/imagegen/contact-v6. See docs/CONTACT-PALETTE-V6.md and docs/CONTACT-PALETTE-V6-PROMPT.json.
